# Privacy Policy — Chess YT Bot

_Last updated: 2026-10-23_

This OAuth application ("the app") requests access to your YouTube account
solely so that the account owner's own automation can upload videos and set
thumbnails on channels they own.

## What the app accesses

- YouTube channel metadata (title, description) of the signed-in account
- Video upload (insert) on the signed-in account's channels
- Custom thumbnail upload for those videos

## What the app does NOT do

- It does not read, store or publish your private video content beyond the
  upload itself.
- It does not share access with third parties.
- It does not use data for advertising or profiling.

## Storage

Access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted-as-secrets inside the owner's
own private GitHub repository CI configuration. They are never transmitted
anywhere else.

## Revoking access

You can revoke this app's access at any time at
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
