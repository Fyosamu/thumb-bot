# WEB-DESIGN ADS — the factory

A full advertising project for the studio: **teams of specialists building
e-commerce and every kind of site, top quality, strong SEO, projects from
$1,000.**

## What exists here

| Piece | Where | Runs how |
|---|---|---|
| Positioning + offer | `concept.md` (this file) | — |
| Ad copy bank (FA + EN) | `ad-copy.md` | for captions, DMs, posts |
| Creative templates (Pinterest-grade) | `pinterest-brief.md` + `make_thumb.py` | thumbnails are generated |
| 10,000 video scripts | `bank.jsonl` (generated by `gen_scripts.py`) | one line = one <1 min video |
| Daily reel robot | `reel_robot.py` | renders today's reel + caption |
| Renderer | the exact same `factory.py` shorts pipeline | captions, mellow bed at 0.10, same voice, portrait 1080×1920 |
| Scheduler | `.github/workflows/webads-reel.yml` | cron every day 14:00 UTC, artifact ready |

## The offer (single sentence)

A dedicated team designs, builds, launches and SEO-tunes your website —
online store, business site, booking, portfolio, landing — from **$1,000**.

## Video format (identical to our shorts)

- Portrait 1080×1920, 7 scenes, ≈30 s (well under one minute).
- Yellow captions, black 75% box, never two on screen.
- Mellow music bed mixed at volume 0.10 — texture, never competing.
- Voice: `en-US-AvaMultilingualNeural` (same as the sample shorts).
- Custom thumbnail: gradient + browser mockup + headline + price badge.

## Instagram plan

- 1 reel per day, rendered automatically at 14:00 UTC and dropped as a
  GitHub artifact (`gh run download -n daily-reel` pulls today's pack:
  mp4 + jpg + `caption.txt` ready to paste).
- The actual auto-post arms the moment an IG account exists
  (`INSTAGRAM\plan.md` covers signup; the upload step plugs into
  `reel_robot.py --post` once credentials are stored as secrets).

## Money/positioning rules

- Price is always framed as **"from $1,000"** — never a fixed number.
- Every video mentions the specialist-team angle + SEO at least once.
- CTA is always one clear verb: DM a keyword, or link in bio.
